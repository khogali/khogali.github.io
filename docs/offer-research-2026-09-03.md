## Top 5 sales-page notes

- **LifeFone**: catalog site, no VSL/timer; $30.95–34.95/mo, free month on annual. Angle: caregiver POV ("Mom lives alone, no contract"). Risks: commission reversed on 30-day cancels; selective approval wants senior content on the site first; Meta personal-attributes if copy implies the reader is elderly.
- **Medical Guardian**: promo codes, "$1 a day", 4.7 Google, product quiz, pricing behind call/quiz (phone-heavy conversions may not hit the postback). Paid search closed; get written OK for social.
- **Bay Alarm Medical**: most conversion-ready: "$27.95/month", "Up to 40% off", "Ends tonight", 15-day trial, 4.8 on 19,710 reviews. Risks: program terms only in a directory PDF; refunds inside trial claw back; affiliate page 404'd, confirm it's open.
- **Trust & Will**: trust-led, no price on homepage, no timer. Prices $199–599 then $49/yr. The pre-sell must sell probate cost and pre-frame price. Avoid death imagery; legal angle invites Meta review.
- **Proton VPN**: clean SaaS page, 30-day refund, free tier (a leak on cold traffic; push the 2-year plan). Case rests on 30% renewals; payback 12–24 months. Confirm Meta specifically, $100 payout threshold.
