# Niche and portfolio research for AI-assisted affiliate content sites

Date: 2026-09-03. Operator: solo, US, full-time job, ~$2k capital, no riba verticals, Vercel + Git + MDX. Five research threads (niche selection, portfolio structure, footprint risk, Google policy, monetisation) run in parallel with web verification; every figure is tagged **[verified: source]**, **[estimate]** or **[opinion]**.

## TL;DR

1. **The premise needs a correction before the plan.** A portfolio of narrow AI-assisted affiliate comparison sites is the exact profile that lost in every Google update from September 2023 through May 2026. Of ~400 sites obliterated by the Helpful Content Update, 22% had recovered 20%+ a year later, and of the 130 hardest hit, 129 never did [verified: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-helpful-content-update-recovery-data-38358.html, https://serps.io/blog/helpful-content-update-recovery]. The March 2026 core moved visibility from comparison sites to primary sources (NerdWallet −15.9%, CreditKarma −34.2%) [verified: https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/google-march-2026-core-update-winners-losers-analysis/].
2. **Build one site, not six.** The evidence favours one focused site with 3–4 adjacent topic clusters, splitting into siblings only after a traction gate (below). Sibling sites that share templates, merchants and a drafting pipeline are "a collection of doorway sites" in Google's own words [verified: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-multiple-sites-ip-not-problem-similar-content/254799/].
3. **Top pick: operations software for solo trades and one-to-five-person service businesses.** Field-service scheduling, invoicing, business phone, formation, and landlord tools. No Wirecutter, Forbes or Healthline in the SERPs, $100–$250 one-time or 25% recurring payouts, and CPCs of $78–$300 on the head terms, which signals real buyer value [verified: DataForSEO pull 2026-09-03, tables below]. Runner-up: creator and newsletter tools (Kit and beehiiv pay 50–60% recurring for 12 months).
4. **Footprint separation is mostly folklore.** Shared Vercel IPs, one GitHub, one Google account and one registrar are irrelevant. Content distinctness per site is the only signal every Google statement and every 2024–2026 case actually turns on.
5. **Realistic money:** a blended B2B software site earns roughly $660/mo at 10k sessions, $4,300 at 50k and $9,600 at 100k [estimate, math in section 5]. Expect 4–8 months before a fresh domain shows meaningful clicks [estimate].

### Avoid

- Generic VPN, password-manager and web-hosting head terms: page one is TechRadar, Tom's Guide, Security.org, Cybernews, Forbes Advisor, PCMag.
- Hearing aids and medical alert head terms: NCOA, Forbes Health, U.S. News, Consumer Reports, plus YMYL; Jabra pays $10/lead and Medical Guardian shows $0 EPC. The DataForSEO pull shows "hearing aids" long tail is Amazon-driven.
- Anything Amazon-linked: 24-hour cookie, 1–4.5% rates, attribution narrowed to the promoted ASIN on 14 Apr 2026 and further cuts in May 2026 [verified: https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-associates-commission-cut-may-2026].
- Low-CPA, short-cookie programs (Talkspace $15–20 with a 7-day window) that don't justify YMYL exposure.
- Notion's affiliate program closed to new affiliates mid-2026 [verified: https://www.way2earning.com/2026/08/notion-affiliate-program/].

## 2. Portfolio structure

### What the evidence says about narrow sites vs one authority site

| Evidence | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Sistrix HCU analysis: 250 losers at −70% to −97%, mostly small informational sites (visibility 0.2–1.0); 220 retail sites checked, none lost [verified: https://www.sistrix.com/blog/google-helpful-content-update-september-2023/] | Small informational and affiliate sites were the target class |
| Gabe's 400-site cohort: 22% recovered ≥20% by Aug 2024; Lily Ray: 129 of 130 hardest hit still declining [verified: https://serps.io/blog/helpful-content-update-recovery] | Narrow sites were hit harder and stayed down longer |
| HouseFresh: −95% Sept 2023, back above peak Oct 2025 (25 months), credited to YouTube, a Linus Tech Tips collaboration and "a lot of luck" [verified: https://ppc.land/housefresh-achieves-notable-traffic-recovery-after-google-algorithm-impacts-2/] | Recovery needed off-Google brand demand |
| Graphite via Kevin Indig: pages with high topical authority gain traffic 57% faster; HubSpot dropped after topic over-expansion [verified: https://www.growth-memo.com/p/how-to-measure-topical-authority] | Depth within a topic beats breadth |
| Fat Stacks 10-site launch: 3 winners, 2 maybes, 5 losers; a 100-post site had "hardly any traffic after 8 months"; advice: launch one site [verified: https://fatstacksblog.com/site-launch-case-study/] | Spreading a solo operator across sites halves the odds per site |
| Onfolio (public multi-site holding company) Q2 2026 revenue −52% YoY, "lacks the scale required to achieve consolidated profitability", pivoting to SaaS [verified: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/20/3348192/0/en/] | Portfolio operators are exiting the model |
| Empire Flippers 2026: "affiliate sites as a whole get deeply wounded by the advent of searchers moving to LLMs"; multiples 26.6x → 23.9x [verified: https://info.empireflippers.com/hubfs/2026%20Lead%20Magnets/2026%20State%20of%20the%20Industry%20Report.pdf] | Exit values are falling |
| Ahrefs 2025: 1.74% of new pages reach the top 10 within 12 months; 72.9% of top-10 pages are 3+ years old [verified: https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-in-google-and-how-old-are-top-ranking-pages/] | Time is the scarce input, and N sites means N sandboxes |

Solo math [estimate]: 10 hours a week is about 40 hours a month. An AI draft with real human review, screenshots, MDX and internal links is 2–3 hours per article, so 13–20 publishable articles a month. One site reaches 150–240 articles in a year. Three sites get 50–80 each, below the depth where any of the Fat Stacks winners emerged (82–496 posts).

Shared leverage is real for build time (one repo, one component library, one legal set, one keyword workflow) and worthless for ranking time. Shared risk is real: the same author, template, merchants and drafting pipeline means one classifier decision replicated N times, and HCU hit whole categories at once (78% of 671 travel publishers lost traffic) [verified: https://serps.io/blog/helpful-content-update-recovery].

### Recommended structure for the top pick

One domain, "operations software for solo and small service businesses", built as four clusters that share a buyer but not a merchant list:

| Cluster | Example pages | Merchants |
|---|---|---|
| Field-service and scheduling, trade by trade | "Jobber vs Housecall Pro for a one-truck plumber", "cheapest scheduling app for a solo cleaner", "how to set up recurring invoices in Jobber" | Housecall Pro, Jobber, Workiz |
| Getting paid and phones | "Nextiva vs Ooma for a two-person HVAC shop", "AI receptionist for a landscaping business" | Nextiva, Ooma |
| Starting the business | "LLC for a solo electrician in Texas: cost and steps", state-by-state refreshes | Northwest, ZenBusiness |
| Adjacent: landlords with 2–10 units | "Buildium vs Innago for four units" | Buildium, DoorLoop |

Gate to add site #2 [opinion, thresholds from the evidence above]: site #1 has 100+ published articles, is 12+ months old, has 5k+ organic sessions a month for three consecutive months, survived one core update flat or up, and the candidate topic fails the overlap test so it cannot live on site #1.

Sibling test: compute top-10 SERP domain overlap for each site's 20 head terms. Over 60% overlap is one site; under 30% can be separate [verified tool: https://lumina-seo.com/tools/serp-overlap/]. Add two gates: a different buyer (if the same person would read both sites in the same month, it's one site) and a different merchant set. Trades software vs creator tools passes. Trades software vs landlord software fails as a sibling and belongs as a cluster.

Candidate siblings once the gate is met, in order: creator and newsletter tools (different buyer, different merchants, recurring), then privacy software for small business (Proton, NordPass B2B, PIA).

Aged domains: expired-domain abuse is a named policy since March 2024 and the test is topical continuity [verified: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies]. At $2k, a niche-matched aged domain eats the budget for a shortcut that now carries demotion risk. Exception: an existing owned domain whose history already matches the topic. khomolab.com is described in the parent session as an existing site in the solo-trades software niche with Ahrefs and Search Console connected; if its history and backlinks match cluster 1, it is the right home for site #1 and should not be replaced with a fresh domain [assumption, not checked in this session].

## 3. Footprint and network detection

| Signal | Grade | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shared hosting or IP (Vercel, Cloudflare) | Folklore | Mueller: "All the same IP address, that's really not a problem for us" [verified: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-multiple-sites-ip-not-problem-similar-content/254799/]. Vercel's 76.76.21.21 is anycast shared by all projects [verified: https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-dns] |
| Same GA, Search Console, AdSense account | Weak; not a ranking input | "Google Analytics is not used in search quality in any way" [verified: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ranking-factors/google-analytics/]. Multiple GSC properties in one account: "It's fine like that" [verified: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-console-properties-multiple-32764.html]. Visible to a human reviewer once you are already under manual review |
| Affiliate IDs | Folklore | No Google statement found; per-site tracking IDs are free anyway |
| WHOIS and registrar | Weak | Private WHOIS "doesn't change anything" [verified: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-private-whois-domain-name-30495.html]. The 2024 leak shows Google stores registration dates, not registrant identity [verified: https://searchengineland.com/google-search-document-leak-ranking-442617] |
| Same template or design | Folklore alone | "Lots of sites use the same themes"; duplicate code across domains: "Nope" [verified: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-seo-duplicate-theme-32469.html]. Identical content is the risk, via canonicalisation |
| Interlinking, footer "our sites" | Weak, scale-dependent | Fine for "5 or 10 sites, maybe maximum" if significantly different; hundreds looks like doorways [verified: https://iloveseo.com/seo/footer-links-to-multiple-sites-are-just-fine-so-long-as-the-sites-are-significantly-different/] |
| Content similarity and cadence across sites | Strong, the actual signal | Scaled content abuse includes "creating multiple sites with the intent of hiding the scaled nature"; doorway abuse includes "multiple websites with slight variations"; thin affiliation covers "cookie-cutter sites or templates replicated across multiple domains" [verified: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies, updated 2026-08-28] |
| Same author name or fabricated personas | Weak for rankings, reputational risk | Author bios are not a ranking factor; fake AI personas caused the Sports Illustrated scandal, not a documented penalty [verified: https://futurism.com/sports-illustrated-ai-generated-writers]. One real name across sites is fine |

What happens when a network is identified: manual actions are per property, scoped "affects all pages" or a URL pattern, with no language about extending to other sites of the same owner [verified: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9044175]. In 2024–2026 the documented hits were algorithmic and site-level: Originality.ai's March 2024 list (1,446 of ~79k sites, ~30% at 95%+ AI) [verified: https://originality.ai/blog/google-march-2024-manual-action-ai-update-list]; Tim Kraft's three identically built AI affiliate sites all "flatlined" in the same spam update with no manual action [verified: https://searchengineland.com/google-spam-update-ai-affiliate-sites-seo-experiment-470168]. They died together because each independently matched the classifier, not because Google linked them. The only network-as-network enforcement on record is the 2012–2014 PBN link-scheme waves.

### Separation checklist

Necessary (this is what gets you hit):
- Distinct niche per site; no overlapping keyword targets; no content reuse across repos beyond legal boilerplate.
- Unique copy per page; real testing, opinion and comparison on every affiliate page.
- Each site stands as a brand: About, contact, editorial policy, a real author.
- No expired domains.
- Sibling links limited to a plain "our other sites" line; stay under ten sites.

Cheap insurance (do if free):
- Separate GA4 and Search Console properties per site, same account is fine.
- Separate affiliate tracking IDs per site, same accounts.
- Vary layout, palette and nav per site; share the component library, not the look.
- Vary publishing rhythm; don't push all repos in the same hour.
- WHOIS privacy on.

Folklore (skip): separate hosts or "SEO hosting", separate Google accounts and burner emails, separate registrars or cards, rewriting CSS class names, never linking siblings, separate GitHub orgs.

## 4. Google policy reality, 2026

Naming check: there is no combined "March 2026 update" or an "August 2026 core". What shipped: March 2026 spam update (24–25 Mar, 19.5 hours), March 2026 core (27 Mar–8 Apr), May 2026 core (21 May–2 Jun), June 2026 spam (24–26 Jun), August 2026 spam (18–21 Aug) [verified: https://status.search.google.com/summary]. August 2026 introduced no new policies; SE Ranking measured 16.71% of top-10 URLs falling past 100 vs a 9.2% July baseline [verified: https://searchengineland.com/google-august-2026-spam-update-ranking-impact-485980].

Scaled content abuse wording (page updated 2026-08-28): "many pages generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users", any production method, including spreading across multiple sites to hide scale [verified: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies]. Google's gen-AI guidance still does not ban AI content and does not mandate human review [verified: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content]. Mueller, Nov 2025: "Just rewriting AI content by a human won't change that, it won't make it authentic... Starting with a bad state will be harder than starting with a new domain" [verified: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-mueller-says-sites-in-a-bad-state-may-need-to-start-over/562109/].

What distinguishes AI-assisted content that ranks:
- Ahrefs, 1M pages, June 2026: 5.3% of positions 1–3 are fully AI; 54.7% are under 20% AI; low and moderate AI pages earn 2–3x the impressions; no binary penalty, gradual degradation tied to quality [verified: https://ahrefs.com/blog/google-doesnt-punish-ai-content/].
- Gabe, Aug 2026 spam cases: an Amazon-affiliate site with scraped merchant copy plus AI padding lost 14,000 queries; a site with 85% programmatic pages was hit site-wide despite human sections [verified: https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/].
- Lily Ray's never-recovered HCU cohort: affiliate-only content with no first-hand experience, assets sourced from Pinterest or YouTube with two or three generic sentences each [verified: https://serps.io/blog/helpful-content-update-recovery].
- Quality Rater Guidelines (Sept 2025 version): Lowest applies when "all or almost all of the MC is copied, paraphrased, embedded, auto or AI generated" with "little to no effort, originality, or added value" [verified: https://searchengineland.com/google-quality-raters-content-ai-generated-454161]. A circulating "June 2026 QRG update" with a "Synthetic Authority" flag is unverified and likely fabricated.

Thin affiliation: publishing affiliate content "copied directly from the original merchant without any original content or added value". No published ratio; Gabe's 85%-programmatic case is the only concrete data point. Keep templated pages well under half the index [estimate].

Human review in practice: there is no evidence Google detects the process. The practical test is whether the page contains things only a person who used the product could write: screenshots of the actual setup, measured pricing at real usage tiers, failures encountered.

Recovery: Google says changes can take "a few days" or "several months" and offers no guarantee [verified: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates]. The June 2025 core produced the first real HCU recovery window, 21 months after the hit [verified: https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/june-2025-google-core-update/].

AI Overviews: zero-click US searches 68% in Jan–Apr 2026 vs 60% in 2024 [verified: https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717]; position-1 CTR down 58% when an AIO is present [verified: https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-google-ai-overviews-click-through-rates-58-study/]; a randomised field study found AIOs cut organic clicks 38% on the 42% of queries where they appear [verified: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-cut-organic-clicks-38-field-study-finds/573145/]. Branded queries with an AIO gained CTR (+18.7%) while non-branded lost 20% [verified: https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/google-ai-overviews-new-research-reveals-how-to-navigate-click-drop-off/]. Commercial comparison queries ("X vs Y for Z") are the least damaged class, which is exactly the content the top pick is built on.

## Critique of this plan
