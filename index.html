Mudroom

Nothing enters your filesystem with its shoes on.

Every file that lands in Downloads stops at the door. You give it a real name and a room, or it waits on the mat where you can see it. Leave it long enough and it gets bagged for pickup. Nothing is deleted behind your back, ever.

Three doors, one mat

Mac appCatches everything that reaches Downloads: browsers, AirDrop, Slack, Mail. The mat lives on the right screen edge. Hover it, or press ⌃⌥M.
Swift, menubar, no network.
ChromeHolds a download at the door before it's written. Pick a room, it lands there. After two sends from the same site, it routes itself.
Works alone, or hands finished files to the app.
SafariSafari has no downloads API, so the extension is a window onto the app's mat from the toolbar.
Needs the app.

House rules

  1. A file is caught only once it has stopped growing. Partial downloads are never touched.
  2. Rooms are learned from what you do, not configured. Rules are visible and editable.
  3. Installers, scripts, and quarantined files are badged with their source domain before they go anywhere.
  4. Seven days on the mat: dusty. Fourteen: bagged. Thirty: the real Trash, from a folder you can see.
