Why afternoon cravings hit hardest after 45, and what actually changes it

A short read on appetite, routine, and the 3pm window.

If your willpower feels fine all morning and then collapses somewhere between two and four in the afternoon, that is not a character problem. It is the most common pattern people describe, and it has more to do with routine than resolve.

The part most advice skips

Most guidance starts at the plate. Eat less of this, more of that. But the people who describe the biggest change rarely talk about the food first. They talk about the moment before it, the stretch of the afternoon where the decision gets made, and about having something steady in place before that window opens.

  • The afternoon window is predictable, which makes it plannable
  • A routine you can keep on a busy day beats one you keep on a good day
  • Consistency is the variable almost everyone underestimates
The pattern is common enough that most people recognise it immediately once it is named. Recognising it is the part that makes it addressable.
