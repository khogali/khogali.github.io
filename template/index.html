What this is

Most advice about eating starts at the plate and treats a hard afternoon as a decision you made badly. We are more interested in the hour before the decision, and in routines that survive a bad night's sleep and a busy Tuesday.

We publish occasionally. Nothing here is a plan, a programme, or a promise.
