# Khogali Transport — Medical Courier Launch

A new revenue line bolted onto Khogali Transport LLC (NEMT), Upper Darby / Delaware County, PA.

---

## 🎯 The whole thing in 6 lines

- **Verdict:** GO — but **not** as a standalone courier company. Enter as a **1099 sub-courier for an existing local medical-courier firm**, running your 2015 Kia Sedona on their routes, under their compliance umbrella.
- **Why:** Near-zero new capital, zero cold-selling, real medical routes in ~2 weeks. You learn the business on someone else's insurance and client contracts.
- **Do NOT:** chase labs/hospitals/dialysis directly (locked up by captive/national fleets), or touch controlled substances at launch.
- **Then:** once you've run routes and have a track record, land **one recurring LTC-pharmacy route you own** → only then stand up your own insured "prime" entity.
- **Biggest risk:** the *margin trap* — going "prime" too early, when ~$5–7k/yr fixed overhead doesn't clear.
- **One next action:** see the bottom of `01-verdict-and-risks.md`.
