## D. Ops SOP (the run, start to finish — Amro/dad run from this)

> **Daily medical courier route — Standard Operating Procedure**
>
> **Before leaving:**
> 1. Check route/manifest in app. Confirm stops, windows, temp requirements.
> 2. Load supplies: UN3373 packaging, cooler + frozen/gel packs if refrigerated/frozen, **temp logger started**, spill kit, PPE (gloves), hand sanitizer, sealed opaque PHI bags.
> 3. Vehicle check: fuel, clean cargo area.
>
> **At each PICKUP:**
> 4. Verify the specimen/package matches the manifest.
> 5. Confirm packaging is sealed and labeled (UN3373 if specimen).
> 6. Place in correct temperature zone (cooler/ambient). **Never** leave in sun/trunk heat.
> 7. **Scan + capture signature + timestamp** in app. Note any issue.
>
> **In transit:**
> 8. Keep packages secured and out of public view. Doors locked when unattended.
> 9. Do not open or inspect specimens. Maintain temperature.
>
> **At each DELIVERY:**
> 10. Hand to authorized receiver. **Scan + signature + timestamp** in app.
> 11. For temp-sensitive: note temp logger reading; attach PDF/log to the run.
>
> **End of route:**
> 12. Submit all PoD + temp logs in app. Flag any exceptions to dispatch (Amro).
> 13. Clean/sanitize cooler and cargo area. Restock packaging.
>
> **If a SPILL/exposure happens:**
> 14. Stop. Use spill kit + PPE. Contain. Do not touch with bare hands.
> 15. Call dispatch (Amro) immediately. Document per Exposure Control Plan.
>
> **If a package is lost/damaged:**
> 16. Call dispatch immediately. Document. (Controlled substances, if ever carried: client/pharmacy must file DEA Form 106 — notify them same day.)
