## 💰 CFO — Unit economics (said loudly)

**Phase 1 is a high-contribution play disguised as a low-wage one.** The reason: the Sedona and your dad are **already owned and currently idle** (NEMT pays you $0 right now). So nearly the entire sub revenue is *contribution*, not offset by new fixed cost.

- Sub revenue: **$700–1,400/wk per vehicle gross** [V]. Midpoint ~$1,000/wk → ~**$52k/yr** [ILL].
- Incremental cost ≈ fuel (~$80–120/wk) + SE tax (15.3% on profit) + ~$40 one-time training. No new vehicle, no new driver, prime carries insurance/BAA/authority.
- **Net new contribution from dead time: ~$35–42k/yr per vehicle** [ILL]. That is the entire CFO case for Phase 1: you are selling idle capacity.

**Phase 2 only works with route DENSITY, not one route.**
- Fixed overhead as a prime: **$5–7k/yr** (one vehicle), insurance = 70–80% of it [V].
- One anchor LTC route: ~$1,320–2,530/mo ≈ **$16–30k/yr** [V daily-route benchmark]. One route *barely clears* overhead after fuel/driver.
- The model is **operating leverage**: every additional route stacks on the *same* fixed base. **3–4 routes per vehicle** is where it becomes a real business, not a job.
- **CFO ruling:** Do NOT capitalize a prime entity for a single route. Anchor route = permission to *start* Phase 2; route #3 = when it's actually profitable.

**Capital check:** Phase 1 first-month outlay is **<$200** (training + maybe a COI endorsement). Comfortably inside the <$2k, halal (no debt needed). ✅

## 🔒 CISO — Compliance & risk (said loudly)

**Phase 1 risk is LOW and that's the point** — the prime is the HIPAA business associate, holds the PUC authority, and carries cargo/E&O. Your only real exposures:

1. **🚨 1099 misclassification of your dad (the sleeper risk).** PA *presumes* employee status; courier is a flagged category. If your dad functions like an employee and you carry no workers' comp, that's **criminal exposure up to $2,500/day uninsured** [V]. **Mandate:** either bona-fide IC paperwork (he controls how/when, works for others, has his own business) OR put him on payroll with workers' comp. Decide before route #1.
2. **Confirm you ride under the prime's PUC authority** — get it in writing. If not, you need your own ($100 + Form E/H) before hauling. [V]
3. **Driver actually completes OSHA BBP + HIPAA before touching a specimen.** No exceptions.

**Phase 2 risk steps up sharply — do not cross without the kit:**
- You *become* the HIPAA business associate → **breach penalties up to $1.5M/category** [V]. Need signed BAAs + written Exposure Control Plan + PHI-handling SOP before the first owned route.
- **HNOA is the financial landmine** — <10 carriers write it, brutal on 1099 drivers [V]. **Get a written quote before committing**, or it eats the route's margin.
- **Hard NOs at launch:** controlled substances (liability + easy drift into "distribution"), and the **regulated-waste stream** (triggers a $500 + $10k-bond + $500k-insurance DEP permit). Specimens *to* a lab are fine; discard waste is not. [V]

**CISO ruling:** Phase 1 = green with the misclassification fix. Phase 2 = blocked until BAA + Exposure Control Plan + HNOA quote are in hand.

## 🧭 CEO/COO — Synthesis & tripwires

**This is a GO because it fails safely.** Phase 1 risks almost nothing you don't already own and can be abandoned in a week with no sunk capital. It directly serves all four constraints: sales-light (you apply to a prime, no pitching), capital-light (<$200), halal (no debt), operator-runnable (Amro dispatches, dad drives from the SOP).

**Phase 2 tripwires — all four must be true before standing up the prime entity:**
1. ✅ One **signed/verbal recurring route** (LTC pharmacy) in hand
2. ✅ A **realistic path to 3+ routes** (density), not just the one
3. ✅ **Written HNOA quote** that doesn't blow the margin
4. ✅ **Misclassification resolved** (IC paperwork or payroll+WC)

**KILL tripwires — if any of these, stop and reassess:**
- A prime demands you carry full $1M + BAA as a *sub* (defeats the capital-light wedge) → try another prime.
- No prime will let you run under their authority AND your own PUC authority stalls → pause.
- Best available sub rate, after fuel + SE tax, nets below ~$500/wk on the idle asset → the dead-time arbitrage isn't there; reassess.
